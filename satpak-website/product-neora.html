NEORA

Anti-scalant concentrate that protects membranes and extends plant life.

NEORA packaging

What it is

NEORA is an anti-scalant concentrate developed to prevent scale formation inside RO systems.

The problem it solves

Scaling reduces flow, raises pressure, and shortens membrane life. NEORA is dosed ahead of the membrane stage to keep scale from forming in the first place.

Application

Continuous low-dose injection ahead of the RO membrane stage.

Suitable systems

Industrial, commercial and agricultural RO plants running hard or high-TDS source water.

Key benefits

Prevents scaling · protects flow rate · extends membrane life · proven across multiple sites.
