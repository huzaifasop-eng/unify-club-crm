Genesys

Re-mineralizes RO water into balanced, drinkable water — in a white-milky liquid-base formulation.

Product packaging photography pending

What it is

Genesys is a white-milky, liquid-base mineral salt developed by SaT PaK to re-mineralize RO-treated water into balanced drinking water. One kilogram of Genesys processes 3,300 litres of RO water.

The problem it solves

Pure RO output strips water of essential minerals, leaving it flat and imbalanced. Genesys restores a stable mineral profile without reintroducing contaminants.

Application

Dosed in-line after the RO membrane stage, in domestic, commercial and industrial mineralization systems.

Suitable systems

Domestic RO units, commercial bottling lines, and builder/mineralized-tap installations.

Key benefits

Balanced mineral output · high treatment yield (3,300 L per kg) · consistent, repeatable dosing · developed and tested for Pakistani source water.

Packaging

Available in PE cans: 2.5 L, 5 L, 10 L, 25 L and above.
