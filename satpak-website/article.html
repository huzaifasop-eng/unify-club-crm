Our water

Not stripped. Not overloaded. Balanced on purpose.

Reverse osmosis takes almost everything out of water, including the minerals that give it body and taste. We put the right ones back, with the same mineral salts we make for bottling plants across Karachi, and hold every batch inside a tight band so it tastes the same bottle after bottle.

The water is treated and bottled at our own plant, the same one you can see in the film above.
