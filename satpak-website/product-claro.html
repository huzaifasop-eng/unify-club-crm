CLARO

Transparent crystal liquid-base mineral salt for balanced re-mineralization.

CLARO packaging

What it is

CLARO is a transparent, crystal liquid-base mineral salt formulated by SaT PaK. One kilogram of CLARO processes 3,300 litres of RO water into mineralized drinking water.

The problem it solves

Where a clear, sediment-free dosing solution is preferred, CLARO delivers the same balanced re-mineralization as KLASSeC in a transparent formulation.

Application

In-line dosing after RO, wherever clarity of the dosing solution matters for visual monitoring.

Suitable systems

Domestic RO units, commercial bottling lines, and builder/mineralized-tap installations.

Key benefits

Clear, easy-to-monitor solution · high treatment yield (3,300 L per kg) · consistent dosing · tested for Pakistani water.

Packaging

2.5 L, 5 L, 10 L and 25 L PE cans.
