PULSER

A dosing-pump reviver, exclusive to SaT PaK, for pumps losing accuracy.

PULSER packaging

What it is

PULSER is a dosing-pump reviver developed by SaT PaK to restore accuracy to chemical dosing pumps.

The problem it solves

Dosing pumps lose priming and accuracy as internal components foul or wear. PULSER restores reliable dosing without replacing the pump.

Application

Circulated through the dosing pump head and lines during a scheduled service visit.

Suitable systems

Diaphragm and peristaltic dosing pumps used across RO and water-treatment systems.

Key benefits

Restores dosing accuracy · extends pump service life · exclusive SaT PaK formulation.

Packaging

2.5 L, 5 L, 10 L and 25 L PE cans.
