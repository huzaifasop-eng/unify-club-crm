Founder

Muhammad Ali Bin Zaki

When I began this work, water treatment was not yet an industry in Pakistan. It was a gap. Families were paying for water they could not trust, and factories were losing entire plants to a problem no one had bothered to solve properly. I did not want to import someone else's answer and stamp my name on it. I wanted to understand our own water, the water that actually runs through Pakistani pipes, and build for it.

That conviction became SaT PaK. Thirty five years later, it still guides every formulation we make and every plant we commission. We never chased size for its own sake. We chased trust. We answered the phone when it rang. We stood behind the plants we installed, and we still service some of them twenty years on.

People often ask why we never advertised. The honest answer is that we never needed to. Every client came to us because another client sent them. To me, that is the only endorsement worth having, and it is one you cannot buy.

Water is not a commodity to be traded. It is a responsibility to be engineered. That belief built this company, and it is the promise I leave with every family and every business that trusts us with their water.

Muhammad Ali Bin Zaki, Founder
