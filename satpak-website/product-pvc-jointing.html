PVC Jointing Solution

A molecular-level PVC bonding solution that exceeds standard joint ratings.

PVC Jointing Solution packaging

What it is

PVC Jointing Solution is a solvent-cement formulated to bond PVC pipework at a molecular level.

The problem it solves

Standard jointing compounds can under-perform under pressure or over time. This formulation is built to exceed standard joint ratings.

Application

Applied to prepared PVC pipe ends and fittings before joining.

Suitable systems

PVC pipework across RO plants, plumbing and general installation work.

Key benefits

Exceeds standard joint ratings · reliable long-term bond · works with standard PVC pipe and fittings.

Packaging

500 ml, 1 L and 2.5 L tins.
