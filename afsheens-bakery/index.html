About Us

Baked with passion, served with love

AFSHEENS Bakery started in a small home kitchen with a big dream — to share the joy of freshly baked goodies with every family. What began as baking for friends and family has grown into a bakery loved by thousands.

Every cake, cupcake and brownie is made by hand using time-honoured recipes, real butter and the freshest ingredients. No shortcuts, no preservatives — just pure homemade goodness.

  • 100% Fresh Daily
  • No Preservatives
  • Custom Designs
